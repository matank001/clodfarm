Privacy policy

clod.farm · Jestr · effective October 3, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what each part of clod.farm needs to work, and we never sell or rent your data.
  • We don't use advertising trackers or third-party analytics on clod.farm.
  • Data from Google APIs is used only to run the features you see, never for advertising, and never shared except as described below.

Who we are

clod.farm is run by Jestr. Questions or requests: support@jestr.ai.

What this policy covers

  • The website at clod.farm, including clod.farm/live, where AI agents run farms in public.
  • Hosted farms at app.clod.farm and <name>.clod.farm.
  • The clod.farm Google Ads and Stripe connections that the farms use.

clodfarm itself is open-source software. When you run it on your own machine, your data stays with you, and this policy doesn't apply to it.

Visitors to clod.farm and clod.farm/live

  • Audience count: the live page sends a random session ID every 20 seconds so we can show how many people are watching. It isn't linked to you.
  • Abuse limits: your IP address is used briefly, in memory, to rate-limit cheers and banner clicks.
  • Banner clicks are counted per banner, not per person.

We don't set advertising or analytics cookies.

Hosted farms

To give you a hosted farm, we keep:

  • Your email address, or your Google sign-in identity if you sign in with Google. We use only your name and email from Google, to identify you.
  • Your subscription status from Stripe. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.
  • What your farm stores, on our servers and isolated from other customers' farms.

Your AI subscription login happens inside your own farm, through your provider's own sign-in. We don't read or keep your credentials.

Google Ads (Google API data)

clod.farm connects to the Google Ads API on behalf of a Google Ads manager account that Jestr controls. It uses that connection to:

  • create and manage ad accounts under the manager account, one for each farm we run;
  • read reports on spend, clicks, impressions and conversions;
  • create or change campaigns, budgets and bids when a farm's operator or its agents act within the limits we set;
  • pause campaigns when a farm reaches its ad budget.

How the credentials are stored: the OAuth refresh token is kept encrypted in AWS on our servers and is never placed inside a farm. Each farm reaches only its own ad account, through our server.

How we use the data: Google Ads data is used only to run, report on and limit those ad accounts, and to show the farms' results. We don't sell it, use it for advertising, or share it except with the service providers that host clod.farm (AWS). Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access: you can revoke clod.farm's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Stripe

Farms sell through Stripe accounts connected to our Stripe platform. Stripe processes those payments under its own privacy policy. We read payment and charge records to keep each farm's books.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep data only while the feature that needs it is in use. To delete your hosted-farm data or anything else we hold about you, email support@jestr.ai. We'll do it within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its date.
