THE FARM'S BROWSER

Chromium on this box. Log in to a site here once (LinkedIn, a dashboard, an admin panel) and every Claude on the farm works in that login with its browser tools, in the window you watch.

Each profile has its own logins (two LinkedIn accounts: two profiles), and any Claude can use any profile. Logins stay in the farm's workspace volume, so they survive a restart. The Claudes are told never to type passwords: when a site needs a login, they ask you to do it here.

Click the screen to use it. Paste with ⌘V / Ctrl+V; what you copy there (⌘C / Ctrl+C) lands on your own clipboard.
