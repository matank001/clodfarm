Open source, MIT. Runs in Docker. Say the name out loud.

Sub-agents that merge

Big jobs fan out. The parent picks up in its own session and merges the results.

Paced on real limits

Stops at 80% of your week and 85% of any 5-hour window, from Claude's own numbers.

Tested before it lands

Your check runs on finished work. A failing check sends the agent back to fix it.

Steer it from your phone

Remote Control keeps the farm one tap away in the Claude app.
